## The Problem

**For restaurants:** The pandemic accelerated an existing growth trend in at-home dining experiences. Restaurants lack the infrastructure, resources and expertise to meet this demand.

**For customers:** Access to the world's best restaurants and chefs is limited to physical location and delivery radius.

## The Solution: Pure Profit for Restaurants

Restaurant Kits works with restaurants to develop recipes and then takes over the full end-to-end production of their kits. We order, produce, portion and sell quality at-home dining experiences. Restaurants are paid an 8–12% royalty fee.

## Market

The global meal kit delivery market was **$7.6 billion in 2019**, poised to grow by a **CAGR of 12.8% from 2020 to 2027**.

Competitor figures (company names appeared as logos in the original deck):

- Revenue of €3.7BN in 2020. Grew its US customer base to 2.6 million subscribers this year.
- Revenue of £49.3M in 2019.
- Revenue of $460M in 2020.
- Sales were up 9% to 1.7 million meal kits in Q1 2020.

Expansion is driven by long-term increased demand for at-home cooking led by millennials, and social distancing trends driven by the recent pandemic. **62% of customers want their favourite brands to start selling "cook or finish at home" meal boxes.**

Source: A Tale of Two Tables, Slerp and KAM Media.

## Why Invest

- We have a founding team with strong roots in the F&B industry, including technology platforms, restaurant operations and meal-kit supply chains.
- Raised investment from 1818, Start-up Funding Club, and a number of leading angels.
- Over 30,000 boxes sold.
- Over 80,000 meals shipped.
- Our network gives us access to the leading brands in hospitality. This ensures we onboard the best kits available.
- We offer every service a restaurant needs to create and sell a kit from scratch.
- Through link redirects from existing restaurant websites and social accounts, we dramatically lower the high customer acquisition costs traditionally associated with meal-kit platforms.
- Our model enables us to go beyond food. We have partnerships lined up with a number of music stars, influencers and leading food figures.
- In negotiations to bring dining experiences to the US.
