Restaurant Kits was founded as a new type of dark kitchen. We produce, portion and distribute meal kits from the UK’s best restaurant brands, direct to your door.

Founded in 2020, we have worked with leading brands in hospitality including Gordon Ramsay, Adam Handling, Calum Franklin and Tom Brown.

We sold 30,000 boxes and over £1M in revenue in 12 months, and were raising £1.6M to continue our growth and expand into more immersive box experiences.

Market size

$7.6B meal-kit market

The global meal kit delivery market was worth $7.6 billion in 2019, poised to grow at a 12.8% CAGR from 2020 to 2027.

Demand

62% want it

62% of customers want their favourite brands to sell “cook or finish at home” meal boxes. Source: A Tale of Two Tables, Slerp and KAM Media.

Positioning

Fully vertically integrated

We sell, portion and produce every kit in-house, at the premium, restaurant-led end of the market. Competitors include Dishpatch, Restokit, Plateaway, Big Night and GF2U.

Growth levers

Margin expansion

Drinks add-ons, lower food costs, operational efficiencies and own-brand offerings. Link redirects from restaurant sites and socials keep acquisition costs low.

Backed by 1818, Start-up Funding Club and a number of leading angels. At its peak the business was raising £1.6M, EIS approved, to expand into more immersive box experiences.

The final course

The kitchen has closed.
The story hasn’t.

Restaurant Kits has served its last box, but what it built still stands. It brought the UK’s best restaurants into 30,000 homes, then went further, with immersive at-home experiences for some of the world’s biggest entertainment brands. This page keeps that work on the table.
