Restaurant Kits was founded in 2020 as a new type of dark kitchen. We produce, portion and distribute meal kits from the UK’s best restaurant brands, direct to your door.

We have worked with Gordon Ramsay, Adam Handling, Calum Franklin and Tom Griffiths, shipped 30,000 boxes in our first year, and went on to build immersive at-home experiences.

Market size

$7.6B meal-kit market

The global meal kit delivery market was worth $7.6 billion in 2019, poised to grow at a 12.8% CAGR from 2020 to 2027.

Demand

62% want it

62% of customers want their favourite brands to sell “cook or finish at home” meal boxes. Source: A Tale of Two Tables, Slerp and KAM Media.

Positioning

Fully vertically integrated

We sell, portion and produce every kit in-house, at the premium, restaurant-led end of the market. Competitors include Dishpatch, Restokit, Plateaway, Big Night and GF2U.

Growth levers

Margin expansion

Drinks add-ons, lower food costs, operational efficiencies and own-brand offerings. Link redirects from restaurant sites and socials keep acquisition costs low.
