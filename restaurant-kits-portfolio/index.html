About

This is Restaurant Kits.

Founded in 2020

We have worked with leading brands in hospitality including Gordon Ramsay, Adam Handling, Callum Franklin and Tom Brown.

The problem

Restaurants lack the infrastructure, resources and expertise to meet the growing at-home dining demand. Access to the world’s best restaurants and chefs is limited to physical location and delivery radius.

Key traction

We have sold 30,000 boxes and over £1m in revenue in the last 12 months. We are now raising £1.6m to continue growth and expand into more immersive box experiences.

Market and model

Restaurant Kits is the premium end of the meal-kit market.

Market size

$7.6B meal-kit market

Global meal kit delivery was worth $7.6 billion in 2019 and was poised for strong growth through 2027.

Revenue model

17.85% GM per box

Revenue model included delivery, restaurant commission, Stripe fees, direct wages, printing, food and packaging — with margin expansion driven by add-ons and operational efficiency.

Go-to-market

Blended CAC of £6.23

We work with existing restaurant brands to leverage their audience, while driving adoption through social, gifting and paid acquisition.
